THE PHYSICS & GEOMETRIC CHALLENGE

Why Depth Upsampling Matters & Concepts Used

Standard color image super-resolution focuses on perceptual appearance; depth reconstruction demands exact physical metric distance and sharp geometric boundaries.

FOUNDATIONS
01
⚡
The Depth Discontinuity Problem

At foreground/background object boundaries (e.g., a chair leg against a wall 3 metres away), naive interpolation blends foreground with background. This creates artificial "flying pixels" and blurred rubber-sheet connections that destroy 3D point clouds.

Boundary Tearing
02
▤
Sensor Voids & Zero-Bleeding

LiDAR and structured light sensors (Kinect) produce 90–95% missing measurements and voids on reflective, glass, or specular surfaces. Naive filters bleed invalid zeros into real metric depths. DepthLab introduces strict zero-bleed boolean validity masking.

Zero-Bleed Masking
03
◐
The Texture-Copying Trap

When using an RGB image to guide depth upsampling, naive filters assume every color edge is a depth edge. On high-contrast flat surfaces (like wallpaper or carpet), color patterns get etched as false depth ridges. DepthLab employs dual-evidence gating to suppress texture bleed.

Texture Suppression
04
◈
Confidence-Adaptive Hybrid Fusion

DepthLab dynamically balances classical edge-preserving solvers (Guided & Joint Bilateral filtering) and deep learning (Residual U-Net). Classical filters dominate near validated geometric discontinuities, while the learned branch regularizes smooth continuous surfaces.

Adaptive Hybridization
