UNDER THE HOOD

One problem. Multiple perspectives.

01Interpolation

Masked nearest, bilinear and bicubic baselines preventing invalid sample bleed.

02Classical vision

RGB-guided and joint-bilateral filtering using structural co-occurrence.

03Deep learning

5-channel RGB-guided Residual U-Net learning high-frequency geometry residuals.

04Hybrid fusion

Confidence-adaptive blending giving classical primacy on sharp geometric boundaries.
